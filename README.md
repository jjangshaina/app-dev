### But one of the series that I really love is..
### **When Life Gives You Tangerines...** a korean series.

*The title of the series is a play on the idiom "When Life Gives you Lemons, make lemonade," that means to turn hardship into something positive, with the added nuance of sweetness and quiet resilience.*

*It is about a story about a spirited girl and a steadfast boy's island story blossoms into a lifelong tale of setbacks and triumphs— proving love endures across time. The title "When Life Gives You Tangerines" was chosen because the story is set in Jeju Island, which is famous for its tangerines, not lemons. The phrase suggests finding sweetness and enduring through difficulties with quiet strength, much like how the series portrays its characters overcoming life's many challenges.*

---
***List of Most Favorite Characters***
1. Yang Gwan Shik - He expresses his love through quiet acts of love. He struggles to communicate his feelings in a world that values silence over vulnerability.
2. Oh Ae-sun - She aspires to be a poet. She represents the voice of a generation yearning for freedom.
3. Yang Geum-myeong - Ae-sun's daughter. She navigates her own relationships while uncovering truths about her family’s past. Her journey is about healing generational wounds.

*This isn't just a love story between two people. It's a love story with life itself— with family, with dreams, with grief, with time, and with the land we came from. Watching two people grow not just together but around each other, like trees shaped by the wind.*
